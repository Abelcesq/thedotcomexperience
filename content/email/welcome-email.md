> ## ⚖️ Compliance note (important — read before sending)
> US anti-spam law (CAN-SPAM) and MailerLite's own rules **require a valid physical postal
> address** in the footer of marketing emails. MailerLite may block the send without one, and
> omitting it can hurt deliverability. You don't have to use your home address — the standard
> privacy-friendly fix is a **PO Box** or a registered business/agent address. Recommendation:
> get an inexpensive PO Box (or a virtual mailbox) and use that. Until then, the email is
> drafted without an address as you asked, but plan to add a PO Box before the first real send.
