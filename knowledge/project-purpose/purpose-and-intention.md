# Purpose & Intention — The Dot Com Experience

## Purpose (why this exists)
To explain human purpose and existence in a way modern people can actually use, and to give
them concrete, repeatable methods that produce real, tangible, meaningful life results —
results that authentically fulfill a person's life-purpose desire.

## Intention (the deeper aim)
To move human existence toward a more **cohesive, steward-driven, responsible, and
visionary** state — where stewardship for each other and for all of life is considered,
honored, and respected. The end-state ambition is for this to become a **modern philosophy**
that humanity can embrace for the betterment of human existence.

## The transformation we sell (the promise)
A person moves from *drifting through the life they were given* → to *consciously creating
the life they want*, by:
1. **Discover** — uncover purpose, values, and the things they don't know that they don't know.
2. **Live** — practice integrity (keep promises to self), put values above circumstances,
   care for the body and relationships.
3. **Manifest** — produce tangible results, abundance, and contribution through love and
   disciplined action.

## Business model (how it sustains itself)
A high-quality, **Disney-grade brand** monetized primarily through **merchandising and IP**
(the "merchandising money model"). Philosophy and free content build the audience and trust;
branded products, experiences, and licensed IP generate income. Quality is the moat.

## Non-negotiables
- The philosophy must be *livable and actionable*, not abstract.
- The brand must be *premium and coherent* — every artifact reflects the standard.
- Stewardship is the ethical spine; growth never comes at the cost of the people it serves.
