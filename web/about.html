About the movement

Experience the Experience

A movement for conscious living — a modern philosophy paired with a community and real, in-person gatherings, for people who want their life to mean something on purpose.

What it is

Life is fundamentally meant to be experienced.

How that happens, where the journey takes us, and what it all means is uniquely different for every person. Each of us is on our own personal, unique journey of discovery — yet we are not alone; we have each other, if we choose to create that reality. Words are only symbols; when they are intentionally practiced, they become lived experiences that transform perspective, identity, relationships, and ultimately the world.

The Dot Com Experience exists to help people turn inspirational words into daily, lived action — and to move human existence toward being more cohesive, responsible, and stewardship-driven, where care for one another and for all of life is honored.

What we believe

Each of us is a multi-sensory being and experiences every moment through our mind, body and spirit. The mind, and how and what we think, matters. Our body, and the manner in which we care for it. Our spirit, and the ways that we nurture and seek to understand the magnitude of how we are a unique part of the non-physical as we are of the physical. Most importantly, how each of us is responsible for ourselves and for everything around us in every moment.

The Dot Com Experience is the movement that reminds us that even when the digital world may seem more impressive, human connection — and the effects it can have on the world — will always be significantly more impactful than any technological advance, and serves our call to stewardship.

We hold this openly and non-denominationally: spirituality is honored as a universal part of being human, open to people of any tradition or none.

  • We are mind, body & spirit — and the spirit is real and must be nurtured.
  • Perspective shapes experience; it matters more than circumstances.
  • Love can be generated intentionally.
  • Contribution is our purpose.
  • Responsibility creates freedom.
  • Stewardship is our obligation.
  • Everything is connected; isolation is an illusion.
  • Authentic, in-person connection is fundamental — it most fully maximizes our capacity to experience the experience of being human together.
  • Transformation comes through conscious experience — and the journey is not linear.

Creed

Every week members will ground in the following commitments:

  • I am responsible for the perspective that creates my reality.
  • I manifest through intention and committed action.
  • Love is the universal, unlimited force within me that I can generate and offer in any moment.
  • Being in contribution will illuminate the path to my purpose.
  • Abundance is the continuous activity of being in contribution.
  • Stewardship is an obligation to myself, to each other, and to all around me.
  • Nothing is separate; we are all connected.
  • I create and I experience the experience.

Code of Conduct

Members will agree to the following Code of Conduct to ensure safety, integrity, and focus on the declared goals and preservation of the community.

  • Treat every person with dignity, whatever their beliefs, background, or tradition.
  • What is shared in the circle stays in the circle. (confidentiality)
  • Listen to understand; no unsolicited advice, fixing, or debating.
  • No selling, recruiting, or promoting anything during an Experience.
  • No romantic or sexual pursuit of members within the circle.
  • Come sober and present.
  • The circle is not therapy or medical care; the Host will point anyone in crisis toward professional help.
  • Members are expected to be in full ownership of their declarations and commitments. If there is an ongoing pattern of lack of commitment or follow through, this can be deemed a serious breach. Self honesty and personal integrity are fundamental shared values that apply to all group members.
  • Anyone can raise a concern privately with the Host, an Ambassador or the Founder; serious breaches may and can result in permanent removal from the community.
