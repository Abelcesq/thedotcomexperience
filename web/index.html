Each of us is on our own personal unique journey — yet we are not alone.

Life is fundamentally meant to be experienced. How that happens, where the journey takes us, and what it all means is uniquely different for every person. We have each other, if we choose to create that reality. Perspective, intention, desire, imagination, emotion, and thought — experienced both individually and as part of one united cosmos — are what make this complex, extraordinary moment meaningful and memorable.

We are made of three parts — mind, body, and spirit. The spirit is as real as the other two, and like them it must be nurtured. To live fully is to tend all three.
