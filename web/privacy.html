Privacy Policy

1.Who we are & scope

The Dot Com Experience (“the Experience,” “we,” “us,” “our”) is a movement for conscious living: a philosophy paired with an online community and small, local, weekly in-person circles called Dots. This Privacy Policy applies to information we collect through our website at www.thedotx.com, our member portal at members.thedotx.com, and your participation in our community and in Dots.

By using our site or becoming a member, you agree to the practices described here and in our Terms & Membership Agreement.

2.Information we collect

We collect only what we need to run the community, deliver your membership, and keep Dots safe. This includes:

Information you give us

  • Account & profile details — your name or display name, email address, and the city you provide so we can connect you with a Dot near you.
  • Phone number (optional) — only if you choose to provide it and opt in to text messages about your Dot.
  • Code of Conduct agreement — a record that you read and agreed to our Code of Conduct when you joined, and the date you did so.
  • Waitlist & interest details — if you join our list before a Dot exists in your area, the name, email, city, and intent (for example, “join a Dot” or “lead a Dot”) you submit.
  • Dot-location requests & Ambassador applications — information you submit when you request a new Dot location or apply to help lead one.
  • Messages you send us — the content of emails and support requests.

Payment information

Membership payments are processed by our payment processor, Stripe. When you subscribe, you provide your card or payment details directly to Stripe. We do not collect or store your full card number. We receive only limited billing data from Stripe — such as your subscription status, renewal date, the last four digits and brand of your card, and a customer and subscription identifier — which we use to manage your membership.

Information collected automatically

  • Technical & usage data — such as your IP address, browser type, device information, pages viewed, and the dates and times of your visits. This is standard information our hosting and logging systems record to keep the site secure and working.
  • Find-a-Dot searches — when you type a city into our map or finder, that city is sent to a mapping and geocoding service so we can show the location. We do not use this to build a profile of you.

3.How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account and membership.
  • Process your subscription payments and renewals through Stripe, and send receipts and billing notices.
  • Connect you with the online community and with a Dot near you when one is available.
  • Send you service messages (for example, account, billing, and Dot updates) and, with your consent, community and reflection emails.
  • Send text messages about your Dot only if you have provided a phone number and opted in.
  • Keep Dots and the community safe, uphold the Code of Conduct, and respond to concerns or breaches.
  • Operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve our site and services.
  • Comply with our legal obligations and enforce our Terms.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for third-party advertising.

4.Our legal bases

Where privacy laws such as the GDPR apply, we rely on these legal bases to process your information: performance of a contract (to deliver the membership you signed up for), your consent (for marketing emails and text messages, which you can withdraw at any time), our legitimate interests (to secure, operate, and improve the community and keep Dots safe), and compliance with legal obligations.

5.How we share information

We share personal information only in these limited circumstances:

  • Service providers. Trusted companies that help us run the Experience and act on our instructions — for example, our payment processor (Stripe), our email delivery providers, our website and member-portal hosting, and the mapping service used to display Dot locations. They may access your information only to perform their services for us.
  • Hosts & Ambassadors. When you join a Dot, the Host or Ambassador of that Dot may receive the limited information needed to welcome you and coordinate your circle (such as your name and the fact that you have joined). They are bound by our Code of Conduct, including confidentiality.
  • Legal & safety. When we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to comply with the law, enforce our Terms, or protect the rights, safety, or property of our members, the public, or us.
  • Business transfers. If the Experience is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this policy.

We never sell or rent your personal information.

6.Cookies & analytics

We use a small number of cookies and similar technologies that are necessary for the site to function — for example, to keep you signed in to your account and to protect forms against fraud. We may also use privacy-respecting analytics to understand, in aggregate, how the site is used so we can improve it. You can control or block cookies through your browser settings; some features (such as staying logged in) may not work properly if you disable essential cookies.

7.Email & text messages

Email. By creating an account or joining our list, you agree that we may send you service and community emails. You can unsubscribe from non-essential (marketing) emails at any time using the link in those messages; we may still send you essential account and billing notices.

Text messages. We send text messages only if you provide a phone number and explicitly opt in. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP. We do not sell your phone number or share it for anyone else’s marketing.

8.How long we keep your data

We keep your personal information for as long as your account or membership is active, and afterward only as long as we need it for the purposes described here — for example, to meet legal, tax, accounting, or safety obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. When information is no longer needed, we delete it or de-identify it. Limited billing records held by Stripe are retained according to Stripe’s own policies and applicable law.

9.Your privacy rights & choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal information, including the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Delete your personal information (subject to legal and legitimate-business exceptions).
  • Opt out of marketing emails and text messages at any time.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing, and, where applicable, request a copy of your data in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent where we rely on it, without affecting processing already carried out.

You can update much of your profile and manage or cancel your membership directly from your account. For any other request, contact us at info@thedotcomexperience.com and we will respond as required by law. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights. If you are in the EU/UK, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

California residents. We do not sell or “share” personal information as those terms are defined under California law. You may exercise your rights to know, delete, and correct by contacting us at the email above.

10.How we protect your data

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect your information — including encryption of data in transit (HTTPS), restricted access, and reputable processors such as Stripe for payments. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we become aware of a breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you and the authorities where the law requires.

11.Children’s privacy

The Experience is intended for adults. You must be at least 18 years old to create an account or become a member, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

12.International users

We are based in the United States, and our service providers may process information in the United States and other countries. If you access the Experience from outside the United States, you understand that your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in a country whose data-protection laws may differ from those of your own. We take steps to protect your information consistent with this policy wherever it is processed.

13.Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the “Last updated” date above and, where appropriate, notify you. Your continued use of the Experience after a change means you accept the updated policy.

14.Contact us

Questions, requests, or concerns about this Privacy Policy or your personal information: info@thedotcomexperience.com.
