1.Who we are

The Dot Com Experience (“the Experience,” “we,” “us”) is a movement for conscious living: a philosophy paired with an online community and small, local, weekly in-person circles called Dots. By creating an account, becoming a member, or taking part in a Dot, you agree to these Terms and to our Code of Conduct.

You must be at least 18 years old to become a member.

2.Membership & what it includes

Membership is $20 per month (pricing may be adjusted by country so it stays within reach). Your membership includes, from the day you join:

  • Access to the online community and member connections worldwide.
  • The Creed and the weekly reflection.
  • A seat in a weekly in-person Dot near you when one is available in your area.

If there is no Dot in your area yet: your membership still delivers real value from day one — the community and the weekly reflections — and you join a local Dot as soon as one forms near you. You are never charged for a local circle that does not exist; the online membership is what you are paying for until a Dot is available.

3.Billing, renewal & cancellation

Membership is a recurring monthly subscription billed through our payment processor. By subscribing, you authorize us to charge your payment method $20 (or the applicable local price) each month until you cancel.

Renewal

Your membership renews automatically each month. The renewal date and current status are shown on your profile.

Cancellation

You may cancel at any time from your profile’s billing portal. Cancellation stops future charges. Unless stated otherwise, you keep access through the end of the billing period you have already paid for; we do not pro-rate or refund the remainder of a paid month.

No refunds

Except where required by law, membership fees are non-refundable. This includes fees already paid when a membership ends — whether you cancel, or we suspend or remove you under Section 5.

4.The Code of Conduct (required)

Membership requires your explicit agreement to the Code of Conduct. When you join, you must check a box confirming you have read and agree to it. It exists to protect the safety, integrity, and focus of every circle.

You agree to the following, in every Dot and across the community:

  • Treat every person with dignity, whatever their beliefs, background, or tradition.
  • What is shared in the circle stays in the circle. (confidentiality)
  • Listen to understand; no unsolicited advice, fixing, or debating.
  • No selling, recruiting, or promoting anything during an Experience.
  • No romantic or sexual pursuit of members within the circle.
  • Come sober and present.
  • The circle is not therapy or medical care; the Host will point anyone in crisis toward professional help.
  • Be in full ownership of your declarations and commitments. An ongoing pattern of lack of commitment or follow-through can be deemed a serious breach. Self-honesty and personal integrity are fundamental shared values that apply to all members.
  • Anyone may raise a concern privately with the Host, an Ambassador, or the Founder; serious breaches may result in permanent removal from the community.

The Creed and the full Code of Conduct are published on our About page and are incorporated into these Terms by reference.

5.Suspension, removal & forfeiture of fees

We may suspend or permanently remove any member who violates these Terms or the Code of Conduct, who poses a risk to the safety or integrity of a circle, or whose conduct is a serious breach as described above. Depending on the circumstances, removal may be immediate.

Forfeiture of fees. If your membership is suspended or terminated because you violated these Terms or the Code of Conduct, any membership fees already paid are forfeited and will not be refunded, and no further access or benefit is owed to you. This is in addition to our general no-refund policy in Section 3.

Removal from the community also ends your license to use the member materials, the community spaces, and any Dot.

6.Dots, Hosts & Ambassadors

Each Dot is chartered, not improvised. New Dot locations are created through an application and Ambassador-training process so that every Dot keeps the same format, content, and standards. This protects the community from rogue or copycat groups that misuse the name, the method, or members’ trust.

Requesting a new Dot location is an application, not a guarantee. Ambassadors and Hosts are approved by us, may be required to complete training, and must operate their Dot according to our standards and this agreement. We may decline, pause, or revoke a charter at our discretion to preserve the integrity of the movement.

The names “The Dot Com Experience,” “Dot,” the Creed, the method, and related brand materials may only be used by members and Ambassadors in good standing, for their participation as permitted here. They may not be used to start an independent or competing group.

7.Safety & not a substitute for professional care

The Experience is a peer community for reflection and connection. It is not therapy, counseling, medical, legal, or financial advice, and it is not a crisis service. If you are in crisis or need professional help, please contact a qualified professional or your local emergency services. Hosts will point anyone in crisis toward professional help but are not acting as licensed providers.
